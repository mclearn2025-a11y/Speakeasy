Speakeasy Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · McLearn Media LLC, 109 Orr Dr, Brandon, MS 39042 · Mclearn2025@gmail.com

The short version

Speakeasy keeps everything on your own device. McLearn Media does not collect, see, sell or share any information about you or the person using Speakeasy. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What Speakeasy stores, and where

To work, Speakeasy saves these things in the storage of the browser or app on your device:

  • Each person's profile: name, picture, age group, favorite things, level and level check answers.
  • Words, phrases and photos you add, and family nicknames.
  • The Help card: name, emergency contacts, other important info, and a home address if you enter one.
  • Settings, and counts of words used for the Dashboard.

This information stays on that device. It is not sent to McLearn Media or to anyone else.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send it:

  • Call, Text, Video and Email buttons on the Help card open your phone's own apps with a short message. You see the message and choose to send it.
  • Location is added to a Help text or email only if a caregiver turns that on, and only if your device allows it.
  • Backup files are saved wherever you choose to put them.

Internet use

Speakeasy works offline. When it is online, it checks the website it came from for updates. Like any website, that hosting service may keep basic server records, such as the device's internet address. Fonts, symbols and photos are built into Speakeasy, so it does not contact other companies while you use it.

If you open Speakeasy through a Claude preview link, that page is hosted by Anthropic and loads its fonts from Google. For the most privacy, use Speakeasy from its own web address.

Children's privacy

Speakeasy is made for children and adults who communicate with pictures and words. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Caregivers control all of the information on the device.

Camera and microphone

Speakeasy does not use the camera or microphone. Photos you add are ones you choose from your device, and they stay on it.

Deleting information

  • To delete one person, open their profile and tap Delete this person.
  • To delete everything, clear the browser's data for Speakeasy, or remove the app from the home screen and clear its data.
  • Backup files you saved are yours. Delete them wherever you keep them.

Changes to this policy

If Speakeasy ever offers an optional account to use the same information on more than one device, we will update this policy first. Nothing will be uploaded unless a caregiver turns that on.
